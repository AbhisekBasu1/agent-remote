# Code of Conduct

Agent Remote contributors and maintainers commit to a welcoming, harassment-
free community regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable technical feedback, accept
correction gracefully, and keep discussions focused on the project. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct
issues privately through the same channel described in `SECURITY.md`; do not
post sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest
and protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical.
